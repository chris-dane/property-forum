What the Forum is

The Property Forum was established in 2019 to bring together experts from across the property sector to share knowledge, provide advice and provoke discussion.

We tackle the big topics, from the planning system and government policy, land and development, to property investment and management. We address the challenges the sector faces and provide a positive platform for change.

The Forum is run by property marketing and events specialist Jo Bruce, in collaboration with experts and professionals from across the sector. Whether you want to stay ahead of the market or build relationships in the industry, this is where the property world comes together.

Good to know

How do I get into events free?
After you join, Jo emails you a member code within a day. Enter it on Eventbrite when you book and the member ticket appears at £0.
Can my firm pay?
Yes. Use the company card at checkout and you'll get a VAT invoice by email every time you're billed.
Can I cancel?
Any time, from the manage membership page. Your membership runs to the end of the period you've paid for.
What if I can't make many events?
Then membership is mostly about keeping the Forum going, and the write-ups still land in your inbox. If that's not for you, a ticket on the night is fine.
Bringing the whole team, or want your name on an event?
That's sponsorship rather than membership. Talk to Jo.
